# Tagcast 1.7.0 guide captures — 11 November 2026

Thirty-two originals supplied by the maintainer, imported byte for byte with their
complete frames and existing redactions. They extend the earlier nineteen current
interface/visualization captures. No account writes were performed to import them.
Drafts, reviews, ready-to-transfer states and confirmed history are distinguished
in the inventory. The blank sign-in window belongs to iBroadcast, not Tagcast.
The Mini Player and narrower album window keep their actual native dimensions.

Artwork, photographs, iBroadcast branding and provider text retain their original
attribution and belong to their respective owners. The Ellington album excerpt
has a separate [CC BY-SA attribution notice](1001-background-LICENSE.md).
No cropping, new redactions, retouching or generated UI was applied.
