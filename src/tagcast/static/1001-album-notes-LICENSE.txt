1001 Albums: offline album background texts

These texts are excerpts attributed to Wikipedia contributors by the Album Summary
sections of 1001albumsgenerator.com, with four reviewed album-specific paraphrases
from Wikipedia. They are not book excerpts or user reviews.

Legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en

Each entry in 1001-album-notes.json retains:
- source_url: the Wikipedia article (with its linked contributor history)
- captured_from: the page on 1001 Albums Generator from which it was captured
- captured_at: capture date and time
- attribution and license_url
- modified/excerpt: changes made for this offline presentation
- editorial_reason, where present: a reviewed replacement of an unrelated summary;
  source_url identifies the article used for that adapted text. The original capture
  URL/date remain recorded separately. The review file retains the replacement text.

Changes: plain text extraction, normalized whitespace and sentence spacing,
and shortening to an excerpt of at most 200 words. Four entries use reviewed,
album-specific paraphrases; two have editorial context about their reference title
or partial coverage. No endorsement is implied.
The texts retain their CC BY-SA license independently of Tagcast's MIT code.
Please retain this notice, per-entry attribution and the license links when
redistributing or adapting these texts. The album view displays source and
license links next to each excerpt.
